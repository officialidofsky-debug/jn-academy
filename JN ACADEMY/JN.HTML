Who We Are

Excellence in Every Classroom

Jag Narayan Academy is a leading CBSE-affiliated school in Kanpur, dedicated to holistic development of students from Class I to X.

Our Mission & Vision

We believe every child carries infinite potential. Our mission is to nurture academic brilliance, moral values, and creative thinking in a supportive environment that inspires lifelong learners and responsible citizens.


With state-of-the-art labs, experienced teachers, and a curriculum designed to meet modern challenges — we prepare students not just for exams, but for life.

📚

CBSE Affiliated Curriculum

Comprehensive curriculum from Class I to X following CBSE guidelines with regular updates.

🔬
